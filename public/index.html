KNOW YOUR DUNGEON

Find any EXIT to descend. Nests keep making monsters: destroy them or fight your way past. On depth six, defeat the Drowned King first.

GOLD KEYOpens one gate. Shared by your crew.

SEAWEED PLATE+48 health. Saved until you need at least 24. Crews of 3–4 get two servings.

PEARL TREASURE+250 crew score.

MAGIC POTION +1 special charge. Press Q to use it.

CORAL NESTKeeps making monsters. Shoot it!

WHIRLPOOL EXITSwim into any exit. On depth six, defeat the king first.

KNOW YOUR ENEMIES

Green, yellow, and red pips mean strength 1, 2, or 3. Ranger shots take 1, 2, or 3 hits. Puffer shots hit twice as hard; Lion and Oracle shots also hit harder. Stronger nests make stronger monsters; later depths spawn faster.

GHOST EELFast rush. One painful hit, then it disappears.

CRAB GRUNTSlow crowds. Keeps clawing at close range.

FIRE JELLYShoots along clear corridors. Its bolts hurt more than its bite.

ROCK LOBBERFlees and throws over walls. Move out of orange landing circles!

SHADE SQUIDShots pass through while invisible. Fire when it appears.

MANTA REAPERDrains health. Only Q magic can kill it.

Hold fire to stand still and shoot in eight directions. WASD moves. Hold arrow keys to aim and fire; combine two arrows for diagonals. Mouse aims; click or Space fires. WASD never changes your aim. All aim snaps to 45 degrees. Release arrows and fire to run. For co-op, CREATE CREW and share the code or invite link. On phones, hold FIRE to aim at the nearest visible enemy.
